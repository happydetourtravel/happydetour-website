Your Journey

Seven nights exploring some of the most beautiful ports in the Western Caribbean

🚢
DAY 1

Galveston, Texas

Embarkation day! Board the beautiful MSC Seascape and settle into your home for the next week.

🏝️
DAY 3

Costa Maya, Mexico

Pristine beaches, ancient Mayan ruins, and turquoise waters. Perfect for snorkeling or just relaxing.

🤿
DAY 4

Isla de Roatán, Honduras

One of the world's best diving spots with incredible coral reefs and laid-back island vibes.

🌴
DAY 5

Cozumel, Mexico

Shopping, authentic Mexican food, beaches, and adventure. Something for everyone.

🚢
DAY 8

Back to Galveston

Disembarkation. Return home with memories and maybe a few souvenirs.
