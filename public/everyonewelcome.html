The Journey

Where we’re going

Three ports, two sea days, and a whole lot of nothing on your schedule if that’s what you want.

🚢
Day 1 — Embarkation

Galveston, Texas

Board the Seascape, drop your bags, and figure out where the pool bar is. That’s the whole agenda.

🏝
Day 3

Costa Maya, Mexico

Pristine beaches, turquoise water, and ancient Mayan ruins nearby if you want to explore. Or just find a chair and a drink. Both are valid.

🦦
Day 4

Isla de Roatán, Honduras

One of the best diving spots in the world. Even if you don’t dive, the island is beautiful and the beaches are worth the stop.

🏜
Day 5

Cozumel, Mexico

Shopping, great food, beaches, snorkeling. Something for everyone. Cozumel never disappoints.

🚢
Day 8 — Home

Back to Galveston

Disembarkation day. You’ll leave with a tan, a lot of memories, and probably a few souvenirs you didn’t plan on buying.
