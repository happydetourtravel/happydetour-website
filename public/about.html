About Happy Detour Travel

The Anti-Second Job.

Affordable vacations. Fun experiences. Everyone welcome.

Hi, I'm Eric. I'm here to make travel simple.

For as long as I can remember (definitely since at least 2017) I've been "that guy" in the group chat. I was the one finding the weirdly good flight deals, obsessing over cruise deck plans, and basically acting as an unpaid travel advisor for my friends and family just for the fun of it. I didn't do it for the thanks. I did it because I genuinely love the puzzle of a perfect itinerary.

Eventually, the hints from everyone I knew became too loud to ignore: "Eric, you're already doing the work. You should actually do this for real."

So, I listened. Happy Detour Travel started as a late-night idea in late 2025 and officially came to life in March of 2026. I'll be the first to tell you that I'm still building this dream. There is a learning curve, and some days I'm leaning on a lot of Dunkin' iced coffee, a few Coke Zeros, and a healthy dose of determination to figure out the technical side of the house from my home base in Ohio. But I am working through it every single day to build a travel brand that is vibrant, fun, and something I'm actually proud of.

"Sometimes the best journeys begin with a Happy Detour."

— Eric Carney, Happy Detour Travel

I believe the world is far too big to stay in one spot. You only live once and most of us don't see nearly enough of what's out there. I'm a cruiser at heart. I love that the biggest decision of the day is which pool deck to lounge on. But I've also spent time navigating London and getting lost in the "everything is included" bliss of Caribbean resorts. One trip is never enough. There's always more to see.

Right now, I'm deep in the weeds researching a safari in Tanzania. It's a major bucket list item, and I'm going to make it happen soon.

When you work with me, you're not dealing with a massive, faceless call center or a robot. You're getting me, Eric, in your corner. I'm looking for the right trip for your life, not just whatever is on sale this week. I won't always have every single answer immediately, but I will work with you until we get it right. No high-pressure sales tactics. Just honest help from someone who truly wants you to get out there and see something amazing.

How We Work Together

Travel planning is a conversation, not a transaction.

I'm not a magician, and I'm probably not going to hit the "perfect trip" on the very first try. But that's okay. That's not how good travel planning works anyway.

You'll still be doing the fun research, scoping out excursions and dreaming about the views, while I handle the tedious parts. Think of me as your filter. You tell me what you're looking for, and I do the deep-dive research, check the logistics, and navigate the bookings. We'll go back and forth until the itinerary feels exactly right.

No pressure. No sales tactics. Just honest teamwork.
